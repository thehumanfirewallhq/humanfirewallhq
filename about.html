THE ORIGIN OF @HUMANFIREWALLHQ

We witnessed countless small businesses, parents, and freelancers fall victim to preventable cyber attacks simply because corporate compliance training was boring, elitist, and overflowing with acronyms. Traditional security training treats humans as the weakest link. We believe the exact opposite: an educated, calm human being is the most resilient security layer in existence.

"Technology alone cannot protect you. When firewalls fail and passwords leak, human awareness is your primary and most powerful layer of defense."

OUR OPERATOR COMMITMENT

Every masterclass, checklist, and intelligence briefing we publish is engineered with three uncompromising rules:

  • 01. Zero FUD: No fear-mongering headlines. We focus 100% on practical, step-by-step actions that reduce your attack surface.
  • 02. Zero Tracking: We refuse to deploy tracking cookies, pixel beacons, or invasive third-party scripts.
  • 03. Real-World AI Defense: Every protocol is stress-tested against active 2026 threats, including generative AI voice clones, Teams vishing, and automated prompt-injection malware.
